ACRDX contract map

Every contract that holds one of the three numbers in the brief, and how a price and a redemption travel between them. Ethereum mainnet, read 2026‑09‑22.

1. Who holds what

ACRDX is not one contract. Shares live in the token, the settlement price lives in the Spoke, cash lives in the escrow, and the hub keeps a second ledger that is supposed to mirror all three. Chronicle is a separate pipe that never touches Centrifuge's contracts.

2. Why there are two price pipes

Centrifuge's price is the issuer talking to its own system. Chronicle's price is an independent witness for people who cannot trust the issuer's key: lending markets that take ACRDX as collateral, and your monitor. In August the left pipe stalled for eleven days and the right one did not.
